Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

D&O Property Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with D&O Property Group LLC through our website, by phone, by text message, or in person, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address(es) you own or are inquiring about
  • Information about the property's condition and your selling timeline
  • IP address and basic device information when you visit our website
  • Records of conversations and SMS messages exchanged with us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries about selling your property
  • To send you SMS text messages, emails, or phone calls regarding your property and our services
  • To prepare and present cash offers
  • To schedule property visits and appointments
  • To maintain internal records and comply with applicable laws
  • To improve our services and customer experience

3. SMS / Text Messaging

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from D&O Property Group LLC, your phone number and consent record are stored securely in our system. Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. We will honor opt-out requests immediately and permanently. You may reply HELP for help, or contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

We do not share, sell, or rent SMS opt-in data or consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers collected for SMS communication will only be used by D&O Property Group LLC for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. How We Share Your Information

D&O Property Group LLC does not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate our business (such as CRM software, SMS providers, and email services), under confidentiality obligations
  • With title companies, attorneys, escrow agents, or other third parties necessary to complete a real estate transaction you have requested
  • When required by law, subpoena, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of D&O Property Group LLC or others

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable physical, administrative, and technical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with applicable laws, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. SMS opt-in records are retained for a minimum of four (4) years to demonstrate compliance with applicable regulations.

7. Your Choices

  • Opt out of SMS: Reply STOP to any message at any time.
  • Opt out of email: Use the unsubscribe link in any email or contact us directly.
  • Request access or deletion: Contact us using the information below to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to request its deletion (subject to legal recordkeeping requirements).

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.
